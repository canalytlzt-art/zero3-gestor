---
name: Monthly operational goals
description: Scope and coverage rules for occupancy and aggregator goals in ZERO3 PRO.
---

Store general monthly targets and modality-specific targets as separate scopes. When a modality is selected, compare only its target; never add general and modality targets together. For multiple full months, compare only when every selected month has a target. A custom interval that includes part of a month keeps its actual data but is not automatically compared with a full-month target. Missing aggregator attendance remains unknown rather than zero.

**Why:** overall and modality results represent different populations, and partial goal coverage would make an achievement percentage misleading.

**How to apply:** keep target scope and coverage aligned in goal forms, API responses, charts, and exports.
